# Whole mock seasons

40 seasons played from Day 1 until the show ended, with every empty place filled by a newcomer at the start of each week (20 per configuration: the default mock cast and a violent one, as in `tests/balance.py`). Regenerate with `python tests/season.py --write` (a few minutes). For curiosity, not a gate.

## Notes on this run (2026-10-18, after phase 7)

These notes are written by hand and are not regenerated.

- With the weekly refill, seasons run about twice as long as without it (median 28 days in the default configuration, against 15.5), and about 15 people die in a season instead of 6.
- Almost every season still ends through killing: TereVi's endgame when three or fewer are left, or the Reckoning. In an earlier run of the same script, 2 of 20 default seasons ended with TereVi shut down; in this run none did.
- The ways out need every step of a route at 100%. The furthest route ended at about 50 to 60% on average, and the best got to 99%. They are reachable but rare.
- Newcomers win about as often as the original cast for their numbers: 18 of the default survivors were newcomers.
- Killers who get away after a wrong verdict count as "escaped the island": they leave alive.
- After the 8 newcomer sketches run out, later newcomers reuse those personalities under new names.

## Notes on this run (2026-10-18, after phase 7)

These notes are written by hand and are not regenerated.

- Every season ended through killing: TereVi's endgame when three or fewer were left (39 of 40) or the Reckoning (1). None ended through a way out.
- The ways out (the truth broadcast, shutting TereVi down, the raft, the ultimatum) need every step of a route at 100%. Over three checked default seasons of 10 to 21 days, the best route reached 15 to 47%. At this pace a route needs about 40 days, and a season lasts about 15.
- Killers who get away after a wrong verdict count as "escaped the island": they leave alive.

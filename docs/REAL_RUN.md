# A real-model run (2026-10-18)

One ordinary day (day 3), the introductions, the reveal and the last call, and a staged murder (Vixen kills Noah with a knife) with its trial and remembrance, all voiced by the real model through the `claude` command. 109 calls in about 9 minutes: 95 for the day, 6 for the three gatherings, 8 for the trial and remembrance. Excerpts, lightly trimmed; whispers left out.

For curiosity: it shows what the voices do with the engine's beats. In the staged trial people had no record of where they had been (they were placed, not walked), so nearly everyone says they do not remember.

After this run, three things were fixed: whispers now vary, the last call gives people different stances (drawing lots, bargaining with TereVi, protecting a friend, a dark joke, watching everyone's hands), and an executed killer is remembered as a betrayal, not mourned like a victim.
